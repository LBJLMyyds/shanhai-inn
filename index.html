常见问题 · FAQ

如何恢复已购买的完整版？

打开游戏 → 设置 → 恢复购买。需使用当初购买时的 Apple 账户。

How do I restore the Full Game?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

怎么切换中英文？

设置 → 语言。

How do I switch language?

Settings → Language.

换手机后进度还在吗？

目前存档只保存在本机，换设备需从头开始。

Will my progress move to a new device?

For now, saves are stored on this device only.
